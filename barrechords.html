The long capsule = your index finger flattened across the strings (the “barre”). Dots are the other fingers (1 = index · 2 = middle · 3 = ring · 4 = pinky) · ✕ = don't play that string. A “3fr” label means the chart starts at the 3rd fret instead of the nut. Slide a shape to a new fret and the root note renames the chord — same shape, every key.
💡 On screen you can tap any chord to hear it.

E-shape barres — root on the low E string

Barre + E shape = major · barre + Em shape = minor. F and G are rock's workhorses.

A-shape barres — root on the A string

Barre + A shape = major · barre + Am shape = minor. B, Bm and C♯m turn up constantly.

Root-note map — every barre chord on the neck

Put the barre at the fret shown; the E-shape or A-shape behind it makes it major, the Em/Am shape makes it minor (add “m”).
